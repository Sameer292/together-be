# Reversible MVP defaults

- Email/password with email code verification and password reset. Social providers are deferred.
- Local development may use PostgreSQL-backed Auth and disk media; production rejects that mode. Supabase Auth/Storage can be paired with local PostgreSQL during development. The two Auth stores do not share accounts.
- Proof may contain text, HTTPS link, and private image attachments. Completion criteria are instructions; valid format and deadline make a submission accepted. No judging gate, video, URL fetching, or preview generation.
- Active members create draft or immediate challenges. Owners manage invitations and members. There are no group join limits beyond each group's configured capacity.
- Publication snapshots active memberships. Current active membership must match the snapshot's membership ID. Every participant, including creators and owners, must submit to reveal. One accepted submission is immutable. Deletion revokes future reveal. Missing/skipping never unlocks; an accepted participant can read after the deadline while still eligible.
- Cancellation leaves challenge metadata and accepted proof readable to eligible users. Archive leaves historical group, challenge, and unlocked feed reads available. Both prohibit new submissions and uploads; cancel/archive prevent new comments and reactions. Owners may manage membership of an archived group; members may leave; authors may remove their own reactions, comments, and submissions for privacy.
- One payer sponsors one selected group per subscription, with one active sponsor per group. The upgrade stays bound after payer departure but never gives the former member content access. No self-service rebind. Expiry preserves existing people/content and applies free limits only to new joins/premium actions.
- `FREE_GROUP_CAPACITY`, `PAID_GROUP_CAPACITY`, `FREE_ACTIVE_CHALLENGES`, `PAID_ACTIVE_CHALLENGES`, `MAX_IMAGE_BYTES`, and `MAX_ATTACHMENTS_PER_SUBMISSION` are required policy choices in production. `.env.example` contains local example values, not prices or approved commercial limits. No subscription price is embedded in this backend.
- A single backend process and one PostgreSQL worker are the initial deployment shape. Notifications use a test transport that emits a generic activity event without proof; a production push adapter and device registrations remain external work.
